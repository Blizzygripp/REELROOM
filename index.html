WATCH TOGETHER

Pick videos from your computer. Host from your PC and send a friend a code — you both control playback, and the video streams straight from your machine to theirs.

Hosting: click Add videos and select as many files as you want at once (Ctrl+A in the picker grabs them all). They auto-group into shows by filename — name files like ShowName S01E01 and each show sorts itself.

Joining: just paste the code your friend sends — no files needed on your end.

Heads up: works great on home Wi-Fi. Some strict office/mobile networks block the direct connection.
